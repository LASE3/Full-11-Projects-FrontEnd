58 Years of Metrology Heritage

From Soviet Precision Optics to Digital Enterprise

Established in 1968 in Almaty, Kazakhstan, VOSTOKPRIBOR has evolved continuously through six distinct eras of industrial transformation.

1968 • Foundation

Soviet Optics & Geodetic Instruments

Founded in Almaty as a specialized precision optics manufacturer supplying high-accuracy surveying theodolites, optical levels, and lens arrays for industrial geodesy.

1991 • Post-Soviet Resilience

Supply Chain Independence

Following the dissolution of centralized distribution, the plant transitioned from purely domestic fabrication to international multi-brand distribution and regional metrology support.

2004 • Restructuring

Privatization & Global Logistics JSC

Reorganized under Vostokpribor Global Logistics & Supply JSC, establishing dedicated bonded warehousing hubs and an international B2B technical procurement framework.

2013 • Customer Self-Service

Launch of Customer Portal (System 03)

Large industrial accounts demanded live visibility into project stages, electronic calibration certificates, and billing ledgers, leading to the creation of the self-service portal.

2026 • Current Era

Interconnected 11-System Ecosystem

Fully integrated digital industrial enterprise connecting 95 employees, 60 industrial clients, and 11 distinct web platforms under a singular, hardened security and design baseline.
